阅读目标 Reading goal

练习从具体文本细节提出问题，并用原文依据支持解释。 Practice asking questions about specific textual details and supporting interpretations with evidence.

观察方向示例 Sample observation prompts

  • 区分文言小序与白话日记，观察两部分如何引导读者理解叙述者。 Distinguish the classical Chinese preface from the vernacular diary and consider how each frames the narrator.
  • 将“文本明确写出的细节”与“自己的解释”分开记录。 Separate details explicitly stated in the text from your interpretation.

待研究的问题 Questions to explore

  • 日记中的哪些细节影响了你对叙述者的判断？ Which details in the diary shape your judgment of the narrator?
  • 如果暂时不读小序，你对日记的理解会有什么不同？ How might your reading of the diary differ if you skipped the preface?
